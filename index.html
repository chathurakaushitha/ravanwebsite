Fitness & Health Tech · Mobile (Android) & Web

Gym Trainer App

A digital coaching platform that connects personal trainers with their clients — replacing spreadsheets and scattered messaging apps with one connected experience. Trainers build personalized workout and meal plans, track client progress, and manage their coaching business, while clients follow their plans, log workouts, and stay accountable, all in one app.

  • Personalized Workout Plans — trainers build weekly, day-by-day training programs for each client
  • Nutrition & Meal Planning — daily calorie and macro targets with structured meal plans
  • Progress Tracking — weight, body measurements, and progress photos over time, visualized with charts
  • Weekly Check-Ins — clients report on energy, sleep, soreness, stress, and adherence; trainers respond with feedback
  • Workout Logging & Personal Records — clients log every set and rep, with automatic PR tracking
  • In-App Messaging — direct chat between trainers and clients
  • Billing & Payment Tracking — subscription and payment history at a glance
  • Admin Dashboard — platform administrators manage trainer accounts and oversee all clients
For independent personal trainers & small coaching businesses
Education Technology · Web Application

Institute Management System (IMS)

A complete management system built for tuition institutes — purpose-built for the Sri Lankan private education market. It replaces manual registers and paper-based fee tracking with a single system covering enrollment, attendance, fee collection, and class content, supporting institutes with multiple branches, teachers, and batches from one dashboard.

  • Multi-Branch Management — manage multiple branches and locations under one institute account
  • Student & Teacher Management — enrollment, profiles, and guardian/parent contact records
  • Class & Batch Scheduling — organize batches by subject, grade, and medium of instruction, with timetables
  • Attendance Tracking — session-based attendance marking with historical summaries
  • Fee Management — automatic invoice generation, payment recording, and printable receipts
  • Learning Content Delivery — lesson notes, worksheets, past papers, and class recordings
  • Assignments & Grading — online submission with marks and feedback
  • Role-Based Access — dedicated dashboards for Admins, Staff, Teachers, and Students
For tuition institutes & private education providers
Point of Sale & Retail · Desktop (Windows) & Web · Offline-First

Ravan POS

An all-in-one point-of-sale and back-office platform built for retail stores, restaurants, grocery shops, and salons. Ravan POS keeps working even without an internet connection — sales, inventory, and daily operations continue uninterrupted in-store, then sync automatically once back online. Supports multiple business types and multiple store locations from a single subscription.

  • Offline-First Checkout — process sales, print receipts, and accept payments with zero internet dependency
  • Multi-Business Support — tailored workflows for Retail, Restaurant/QSR, Grocery, and Salon/Services
  • Inventory Management — real-time stock tracking, batch/expiry tracking, low-stock alerts, stock transfers
  • Purchasing & Suppliers — supplier management, purchase orders, goods-received tracking
  • Shift & Cash Management — clock-in/out, cash drawer reconciliation, end-of-day (X/Z) reports
  • Multi-Store Reporting — sales trends and performance across all branches in one view
  • Restaurant & Kitchen Tools — table management, order routing, live kitchen display
  • Customer Loyalty — loyalty points, gift cards, and store credit
  • Hardware Ready — receipt printers, cash drawers, barcode scanners, weighing scales
For retail, restaurants, grocery & salons — single-store to multi-branch chains
AI · SaaS · Web Application

BotForge

A no-code platform for building custom AI chatbots for any business. Users create a bot, train it on their own knowledge — PDFs, FAQs, website pages, or pasted text — and publish it in minutes. The bot answers customer questions instantly using that knowledge, can be embedded on any website with a simple script, or shared as a direct chat link. Every conversation flows into a live inbox.

  • No-Code Bot Builder — create and configure a custom chatbot through a guided setup wizard
  • Train on Your Own Knowledge — upload PDFs, FAQs, plain text, or point the bot at web pages
  • Smart, Grounded Answers — responses generated by leading AI models, grounded in your knowledge base
  • Custom Persona & Behavior — configure tone, personality, and instructions to match your brand
  • One-Click Website Embed — add the bot to any site with a simple snippet, or share a chat link
  • Live Conversation Inbox — review every real customer conversation as it happens
  • Test Playground — try out and refine the bot privately before publishing it live
  • Publish Anytime — switch a bot between draft and live with a single toggle
For any business that wants an always-on AI assistant on its website
